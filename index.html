PROBLEMS

Problems & Challenges

Important challenges related to herbal plants.

01

Lack of Awareness

Many people do not know the importance and traditional uses of herbal plants.

02

Chemical Pesticides

Excessive chemical pesticides can affect soil, plants and the environment.

03

Limited Availability

Some herbal plants are not easily available in every area or season.

04

Need for Proper Guidance

Proper guidance is useful for correct identification and safe use.

SUGGESTIONS

Suggestions & Recommendations

01

Increase Awareness

Teach people about the importance, benefits and conservation of herbal plants.

02

Sustainable Cultivation

Encourage sustainable cultivation and home gardening.

03

Reduce Chemicals

Encourage natural methods and reduce unnecessary chemical pesticides.

04

Expert Guidance

Provide proper training and support from experts and institutions.
